iPhone app · live browser demo

Clean up your camera roll with one thumb

Swipe Photos shows your pictures one at a time. Keep the good ones, send the rest to the trash, and delete them all at once when you finish.

  • right →keep the photo
  • ← leftmove it to the trash

The phone on this page works. Drag a card with your mouse or finger, or press ← →. The pictures are drawn for this demo, and this page never touches real photos.

Trash

Tap a photo to put it back

From cluttered to clean in three steps

  1. SwipeEach decision takes a second. If you change your mind, Undo brings back the last photo.
  2. Check the trashTap a photo to put it back. Your library stays untouched until you confirm.
  3. Confirm onceiOS asks once for the whole batch. Photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
